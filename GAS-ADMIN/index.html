Provision Course Materials Folders

Creates one materials container per course and grants it to the course's Classroom group once. Students enrolled later inherit access automatically — nobody re-shares anything.

Teacher already made a folder? Paste its link or ID in the Existing folder column and use Adopt instead of Provision. Adoption registers that folder as the course container and adds the group grants — it creates nothing, moves nothing, and changes no ownership. Students who already made their own shortcut to it are left alone.
